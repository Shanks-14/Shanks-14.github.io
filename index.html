(01) — About

The short version

I'm an MSc graduate in Information Systems Management (National University of Galway) with four years of hands-on experience turning messy operational data into reporting that engineering, product and QA teams actually rely on. My background spans e-commerce retail analytics at Purple Style Labs and gameplay telemetry & QA analysis on AAA titles at Ubisoft — two very different domains, one consistent focus: accuracy first.

I'm most useful in the gap between "the data exists" and "the business can trust it" — writing the SQL, building the validation frameworks, and packaging the results into dashboards and reports that hold up under scrutiny. I've picked up Azure and AWS cloud fundamentals along the way, and I'm currently deepening that with a major project comparing real-time stream and batch processing for predictive analytics.

Outside the day job: I like clean pipelines, well-indexed tables, and reports that don't need a follow-up meeting to explain them.
